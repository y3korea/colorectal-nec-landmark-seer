# Supplementary Table S1. Analysis sequence: what was fixed when, with what knowledge of the data, and how each analysis is labeled

| Stage | Date fixed / performed | Content | How fixed; data seen before fixing? | Label used in this paper | Where reported |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Pilot | August 2026 | Alternative case definition (main codes plus 8246/3 with registry grade 3/4; n = 3,179), all-cause death, landmarks 0/3/6/12/24/36 months, 12-month window; distant-stage all-cause death from landmark 6 (75.9%) close to that from diagnosis (79.1%) | Exploratory run; data seen | Pilot (exploratory) | Methods (analysis sequence); this table |
| 2. Written analysis plan | 16 September 2026 | Main cohort (8013, 8041–8045; positive histology), cause-specific death with other-cause death as competing event, landmarks 0/3/6/12/24 months, 12-month primary window, B = 2,000 (seed 20260917), pre-specified sensitivity list (expanded cohort; unknown cause of death excluded or censored; diagnosis-era strata; 36-month window among patients with sufficient follow-up) | Fixed in writing after the pilot; not blind to the pilot results | Pre-specified (after pilot) | Table 2; Supplementary Tables S8, S11, S12, S23, S25–S26 (era strata) |
| 3. Monthly landmarks and hazard structure | After the stage-2 results were seen (September 2026) | Monthly landmarks 0–24, broken-stick plateau end, piecewise-exponential hazards with three interval contrasts (6–9 vs 3–6; 6–15 vs 0–6; 15–24 vs 6–15), kernel-smoothed hazards, chemotherapy stratification among 3-month survivors, standardization with the original covariate set | Data-driven; chosen after seeing the stage-2 curve | Exploratory (post hoc contrasts) | Figures 1–2; Supplementary Tables S9–S10, S17, S19, S21, S32–S33 |
| 4. Cross-tumor comparator rules | 28 September 2026 (written protocol with change log) | Comparator eligibility (same filters; entry to the main text only if the 95% CI of the 6 − 0 contrast is ≤ 15 pp wide and the comparison is not already published; results reported whatever their direction), primary contrast (NEC minus comparator 6 − 0 change, paired bootstrap), hazard-shape test; pancreatic adenocarcinoma added after the first two comparators were seen, with a written falsification rule (a decline over the first 6 months, upper 95% limit of the 6 − 0 change below 0, with no plateau in pancreatic adenocarcinoma would support a morphology explanation over lethality; a plateau would refute it) | Written before any comparator result was seen (colorectal adenocarcinoma, SCLC); sequential for pancreatic adenocarcinoma | Pre-specified (colorectal AC, SCLC); sequential pre-specified (pancreatic AC) | Table 3; Figure 3; Supplementary Tables S26–S27 |
| 5. External reconstruction | Search 29 September 2026 | PubMed search with pre-written eligibility criteria; reconstruction of three published cohorts (descriptive; no pooling) | Criteria written before the search results were viewed; reconstruction not re-analyzed in the independent re-implementation | Auxiliary (not re-analyzed) | Supplementary Methods S6; Supplementary Table S44 |
| 6. Revision analyses | October 2026 (this version) | Window-width sensitivity and model comparison; equivalence margins (±3, ±4, ±5, ±6.7 pp); Holm/Bonferroni adjustment, global and isotonic (monotonicity) tests; pre-specified expanded cohort and subtype reporting (the pre-specified diagnosis-era strata are reported in Supplementary Tables S25–S26); 2018–2023 era restriction; baseline and treatment strata with time-updated chemotherapy exposure; extended standardization with bootstrap CI, proportional-hazards check and Fine–Gray sensitivity; split-sample calendar validation; common-bandwidth smoothed hazards with fixed-month and window ratios; lung LCNEC as fourth comparator and the morphology × organ 2 × 2 layout (6 − 0 change; and, 8 October 2026, 6–9 versus 3–6-month rate ratios and hazard-shape tests versus NEC); cytology-inclusive comparators; race/ethnicity and county income; first-primary restriction; data-quality and censoring tables | Post hoc, after an internal pre-submission expert review (eight-member panel); all labeled "added at revision" | Added at revision (post hoc) | Supplementary Tables S2–S7, S13–S16, S18, S20, S22, S24–S25, S28–S31, S34–S43; Supplementary Figures S2–S7 |

Footnote: no stage was publicly pre-registered; the dated written plans of 16 and 28 September 2026 are not part of the code repository and are available from the corresponding author on request (Code and analysis-output availability). "Pre-specified" therefore means fixed in writing before the relevant results were seen, not blind to all data.
